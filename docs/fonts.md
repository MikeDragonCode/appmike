# Fonts

Inter Tight (light, regular, medium) and DM Mono (regular), in `assets/fonts`. Both are under the SIL Open Font License
(copies from the `@expo-google-fonts` packages). They are loaded with `useFonts` in `src/app/_layout.tsx` and named in `src/theme.ts`.
Light is for the big figures, medium for titles and buttons, mono for the small uppercase labels.
