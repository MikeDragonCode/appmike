## Full description

Work earns rest. Rest gets booked.

Shift to Life turns the time you spend on work, study or training into minutes of rest, and helps you spend them on purpose. Instead of drifting into scrolling, you book a real slot for the thing you actually want to do, and take it without guilt.

How it works
- Track focused work with a simple timer, or log what you already did.
- Every hour earns rest at a ratio you choose.
- Book rest as a slot in your day: gaming, a show, time with friends, a nap, a walk, anything you add yourself.
- Finish early and the unused time goes back to your bank.
- Close the day in two minutes and leave yourself one small task for tomorrow.

Built to be calm
- No account, no ads, no tracking. Everything stays on your phone.
- No punishing streaks. A missed day does not reset your progress.
- Short check-ins after a few days help you tune the plan to what is hardest for you right now.
- Optional reminders: wind down before bed, close the workday, use your saved rest.

Shift to Life is a planning tool. It is not therapy and not medical advice.
