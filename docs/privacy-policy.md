# Privacy Policy for Shift to Life

Last updated: [DATE BEFORE PUBLISHING]

Shift to Life ("the app") is built so that your information stays with you.

## What the app stores

Everything you enter (your work and rest activities, the time you log, your booked rest, your settings and your notes) is stored only on your device, in the app's own storage. It is not sent to us or to anyone else.

## What we do not do

- We do not require an account or a sign-in.
- We do not collect personal information.
- We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
- We do not share or sell data, because we do not have any.

## Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules reminders locally on your device (for example, the start of a rest slot or a daily "close the day" reminder). They are created and shown by your device. No notification data leaves it. You can change this at any time in the app's settings or in your device's notification settings.

## Backups

The app can create a text backup that you can share or copy yourself. What you do with that text, and where you send it, is up to you. The app does not upload it anywhere.

## Deleting your data

Use "Start over" in Settings, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything the app stored on your device. Because we hold no copy, we cannot recover it for you.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Not medical advice

Shift to Life is a planning tool. It is not therapy and not medical advice. If you are struggling, please talk to a qualified professional. If you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis line in your country.
